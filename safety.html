Safety

Rabbit Music · Last updated: September 27, 2026

Hearing

High listening volume for extended periods can cause permanent hearing damage. Start at a comfortable volume and avoid prolonged exposure to excessive sound levels.

Driving

Do not interact with a phone or vehicle display when doing so would distract you from driving. Use Android Auto and Rabbit Music only in accordance with local traffic laws and safe-driving requirements.

Files and storage

Only grant Rabbit Music access to folders and files that you want the app to scan and play. Keep backups of important files independently of the app.
