Privacy Policy

Rabbit Music · Package com.rabbitmusic.player
Published by Glitched Cartridge
Last updated: September 27, 2026

Rabbit Music is a local music player and library manager. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, what information may be sent to third-party services when you choose optional online synchronization, and how that information is handled.

1. Local music and device storage

Rabbit Music can access audio files and folders that you explicitly make available to the app. This access is used to scan, organize, display, and play your local music.

Your local audio files and music library are not uploaded to a Rabbit Music server. Rabbit Music does not operate a user account database or a backend for storing your music library.

2. Optional online synchronization

Rabbit Music provides optional metadata, artwork, and lyrics synchronization. When you choose to use these features, the app may send limited music-identification information to third-party services. Depending on the operation, this may include song title, artist, album, filename, or other music metadata available to the app.

The underlying audio file is not intentionally uploaded merely to retrieve ordinary metadata, artwork, or lyrics.

3. Third-party services

Optional synchronization may use MusicBrainz, iTunes Search, Cover Art Archive, LRCLIB, and Lyrics.ovh. These services are independently operated and may process network information and query information under their own policies.

See the Third-Party Services page for details.

4. Information we do not intentionally collect

Rabbit Music does not require an account or login. It does not intentionally collect your name, email address, contacts, passwords, payment information, or precise location for its own services. Rabbit Music does not use advertising networks or sell, rent, or trade personal information.

5. Analytics and advertising

Rabbit Music does not intentionally use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Sentry, or similar analytics services, and does not display third-party advertisements.

6. Google and Android platform services

Rabbit Music is distributed through Google Play and runs on Android. Android and Google Play provide platform functionality. No separate Google account, advertising, analytics, or cloud-storage service is required to use Rabbit Music.

If a future version adds a Google service that processes user information, this policy will be updated before or with that release.

7. Data retention and deletion

Rabbit Music does not maintain a central Rabbit Music user database. Locally stored app data can generally be removed through Android's app-storage controls or by uninstalling the app. Third-party services may independently retain request or technical information according to their own policies; Rabbit Music cannot delete records maintained by those independent services.

8. Security

Rabbit Music is designed to minimize unnecessary transmission of user information. Optional online requests are made when you use the relevant feature. No software or network transmission can guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's privacy

Rabbit Music is not specifically directed to children. It does not require an account or intentionally collect personal information to create child profiles or advertising profiles.

10. International users

Rabbit Music may be used internationally. Optional online requests may be processed by third-party services operating in countries other than your own.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page with an updated date.
