Third-Party Services

Rabbit Music · Last updated: September 27, 2026

Rabbit Music is primarily a local music player. Optional synchronization can communicate with the independent services below to retrieve music information.

MusicBrainz

Used for music metadata lookup and identification.

MusicBrainz

iTunes Search

Used as an optional source for music metadata and artwork-related information.

iTunes Search API documentation

Cover Art Archive

Used to retrieve album artwork associated with music releases.

Cover Art Archive

LRCLIB

Used as an optional source for lyrics and synchronized lyrics when available.

LRCLIB

Lyrics.ovh

Used as an optional lyrics source when lyrics are requested and available.

Lyrics.ovh

Information sent

When synchronization is requested, Rabbit Music may send the minimum music-identification information needed for the selected lookup, such as title, artist, album, filename, or other available metadata. The underlying audio file is not intentionally uploaded for ordinary metadata, artwork, or lyrics lookup.

Independent processing

Third-party services may process requests, IP addresses, technical information, and query information according to their own policies. Glitched Cartridge cannot control their independent data practices, availability, or retention.

Future services

If a future production version adds another service that processes user information, this page and the Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
