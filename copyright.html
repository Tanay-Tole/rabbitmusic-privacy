Local music

Rabbit Music does not provide a catalogue of copyrighted music for download or streaming. It plays audio already available to the user through storage locations selected by the user.

Third-party content

Metadata, album artwork, artist information, and lyrics retrieved through optional synchronization may be owned or controlled by their respective rights holders. Rabbit Music does not claim ownership of third-party content merely because it displays or caches it locally.

Copyright concerns

If you believe content displayed by Rabbit Music violates your rights, contact us with enough information for us to understand the issue. Because Rabbit Music does not host user music on a Rabbit Music server, some issues may need to be addressed with the relevant source or rights holder.
